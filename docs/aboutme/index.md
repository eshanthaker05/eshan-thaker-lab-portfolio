# About Me
## My Story
My name is Eshan Thaker and I am currently studying to become a mechanical engineer with a concentration in biomedical engineering at the University of Charlotte at North Carolina. I've always been interested in understanding how things work, especially in the human body. I started college as a biology major, but quickly realized that I needed a discipline with more hands-on work. Instead of compromising, I decided to get the best of both worlds and switched to mechanical engineering with a concentration in biomedical engineering. After college, I plan on using what I learned to design products that can improve people's health. 

## Skills and Experience
Since I've started college, I've joined the biomedical engineering society club, in which I have met a community of like-minded people to surround myself with. Most recently, I've helped create a computer keyboard designed for people with only one hand. This project challenged me to think creatively and solve a problem I've never faced before. In the end, it taught me a lot about what it takes to succeed in the engineering field.

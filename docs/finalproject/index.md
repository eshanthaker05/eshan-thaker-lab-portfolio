## Part 1: Rough Prototype

### Potential Problems 

**Problem 1:** Wire Organizer

When working at my desk, its not uncommon to see a tangled mess of chargers lying off to the side. Whenever I straighten them out, they usually end up tangled again within a few days, making me less inclined to untangle them again. A potential fix for this is to have a holder attached to the side of my desk with holes/slots for each necessary charger. This would make my space a lot more tidy and save me the headache of having to look at a growing amalgamation of wires as I work.  

**Problem 2:** Phone Stand

I like to watch Youtube on my phone when eating lunch at my home. The problem with this is that I have nothing to prop up my phone with, whether I'm at my apartment or out and about. This gets super annoying because I often resort to using my waterbottle to prop up my phone, making it inaccessible during my meal. Also, the lack of friction between the table and my phone makes it easy for it to slide flat. A phone stand would completely solve this problem and make eating meals less of a hassle. 

**Problem 3:** Car Phone Stand

My brother got me a phone stand to put in my car for when I need directions and it worked perfectly for a while. Over the summer, the heat from the sunlight melted the bottom of the stand, making it almost impossbile for it to stick to the dashboard. I've resorted to sticking my phone in the cup holder and looking at the directions like that, but this can be dangerous while driving. A newly designed phone case would solve this problem and would probably last until summer, at which point its possible it may melt like the previous one. 

### Selected Problem

I ended up going with Problem 2: Phone Stand because it is the problem I most often deal with in my day-to-day life. Having to adjust my phone with food on my hands really annoys me and wastes my time. Another reason I chose this project is because I've actually done this project before in a previous lab, but it didn't turn out the way I wanted it to. I never got a chance to fix my mistakes and solve a problem I regularly encounter, so I decided to take this opportunity to do just that.

I wanted to change this by adding a slot in which I can insert a wooden plate for maximum support while remaining lightweight.

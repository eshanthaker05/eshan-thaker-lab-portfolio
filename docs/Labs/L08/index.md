## Problem Statement

### Purpose 

When eating lunch in my apartment, I occasionally like to play a show on my phone for entertainment. I use whatever is nearby to prop up my phone at an angle so that the screen is visible to me with minimal glare from the window behind me. The problem is that sometimes, the phone doesn't stay propped due to the smooth surface of the countertops. Having to constantly readjust my phone while eating food can be extremely annoying, so I decided to solve this problem in this assingment. Before even beginning the research/design process, I knew I wanted my artifact to have the following features:

1) No external buttons should be covered by the artifact when in use.

2) There should be three, equally spaced angle options for the phone to rest at.

3) The whole artifact should fold into a shape that's easy to store.

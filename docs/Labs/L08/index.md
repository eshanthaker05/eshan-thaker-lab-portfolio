## Reading and Writing

When presented with a new idea, people often shy away from asking questions because they don't want to annoy others or appear to be stupid. This small habit can  leave people underinformed about important concepts, leading to more problems in the future. Asking smart questions solves this issue by giving presenters a chance to clarify confusing topics while maintaining a welcoming atmosphere. It reduces the risk of misunderstanding and can even lead to hidden problems being discovered and solved. Smart questions matter because they build on existing knowledge and can appraoch obstacles from angles previously unseen.

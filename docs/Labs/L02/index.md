# A2 – Print Something Small 

## In-Class Research
DfAM - The wall thickness of your design needs to be thick enough to avoid snapping, but thin enough to prevent heat buildup and too much internal stress. The thickness of your design depends on the material and the type of printer you use. 

FDM - One FDM specific consideration is the anisotropic strength of the material. The strength of some materials depends on the direction they are measured. An example of this is wood, whose strength is stronger going along the grain. These materials must be oriented so that high-stress loads are exerted on strong planes.

## Lessons Learned 

- I learned how to differentiate between some of the printers based on their nozzles and filaments. I did this when I was trying to print the G-code from the USB drive and the printer kept returning an error because I had the wrong printer chosen.

- I learned how to change a single dimension of the artifact without changing the other dimensions. I did this when I was adjusting the dimensions to fit the  length, width, and height requirements.

- I learned how to check the filament type on the printer by checking the spool.

- I learned how to edit the printables after I've downloaded them and pasted them onto the plater.

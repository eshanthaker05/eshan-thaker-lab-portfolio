# A2 – Print Something Small 

## In-Class Research
DfAM - The wall thickness of your design needs to be thick enough to avoid snapping, but thin enough to prevent heat buildup and too much internal stress. The thickness of your design depends on the material and the type of printer you use. 

FDM - One FDM specific consideration is the anisotropic strength of the material. The strength of some materials depends on the direction they are measured. An example of this is wood, whose strength is stronger going along the grain. These materials must be oriented so that high-stress loads are exerted on strong planes.
